## Decisions

- Home says “Cricket ka expert?” and “Gyaan hai? Game dikha.” Other headings use the same competitive Hinglish voice. Mini/Full names,3/6 round counts, conditional completion XP, bot disclosure and recovery instructions stay literal.
- Club accents affect identity and action highlights. Day/night, motion and correct/error meanings remain separate. Use club colours can return the default.
- Reflections are decorative. Text, essential control boundaries, focus and selected-state labels work without gloss. No infinite particles or answer-surface effects are introduced.
- Public tags are display prefixes. Private invites, permissions, email and recovery credentials never enter those labels or opponent projections.
- No real-person retention, satisfaction or performance uplift is inferred. Full live multi-account club matches and physical-phone usability research remain separate future work.
