# Three grounds: original art and three review rounds

## Product before images

The home innings remains open at zero trophies. Riverside Park unlocks a first-to-three Chase at 60; Crown Lights unlocks a six-delivery,15-second Superover at 180. These are new local practice formats, not purchasable advantages. A selected earned ground launches from Home in one tap. The optional road takes at most three actions to open, select and play. Rules, score, locks and trophy totals remain semantic interface text outside the artwork.

## Round 1: world exploration

Generated an original three-world concept board with a consistent elevated camera. Gully used terracotta houses, one large banyan tree and an ochre pitch. Riverside used a teal-domed pavilion, emerald oval and turquoise river. Crown used violet floodlights and a gold crown-like roof. The brief excluded text, UI, logos and copied game assets. The goal was a growing sense of place rather than recreating another game's map.

AI design and mobile-review panels inspected the rendered board. Accepted the warm/river/night identities. Criticism: tiny foliage, distant palaces, bridges and elaborate entrances became visual texture at 160px and competed with the pitch. Human recruited focus groups were not conducted.

## Round 2: separate production compositions

Generated three individual 3:2 dioramas from the accepted board. Each brief enlarged the pitch, isolated its silhouette, reserved clear margins and reduced the background architecture. Riverside's three lanterns and Crown's six roof points serve as motifs only; the live HUD carries all exact rules and counts.

The panels reviewed all three outputs. Gully needed a lighter daytime backdrop. Riverside's boats and flowers cluttered the front edge. Crown's playing oval was proportionally smaller than the other grounds, while its facade took too much space.

## Round 3: targeted image edits and final integration

Edited each round 2 image with those specific findings. Gully now has a warm teal daytime backdrop; Riverside drops the boats and simplifies its entrance; Crown expands the visible field and lowers the facade. All preserve their original world identities. Final web exports retain aspect ratio at 960×640:

The images are generated 3D-style raster art. The browser does not download a 3D engine to show them. Existing Net practice retains its interactive ball trajectory and timing mechanics. During timed quiz questions, decorative motion remains suppressed. Mobile artwork is decorative and has empty alt text; the adjacent headings identify every ground. Missing imagery must never remove the Play action or lock information.

## Editable design iterations

Figma's primary account accepted round 1 as a new native board in the existing project. Its Starter call limit then prevented further screenshots or mutations. Rounds 2 and3 therefore use the installed open-source Excalidraw runtime, with editable native elements and locally rendered exports. See `FIGMA.md` for exact evidence, file links and limitations. We do not label those fallback rounds as completed Figma edits.
