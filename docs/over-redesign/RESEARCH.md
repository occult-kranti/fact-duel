# Mini over / Full over: research and independent review

Research date: 29 October 2026. Scope: JHK's first-game entry, earned progression, local titles and voluntary result sharing. This is a small purposive qualitative source sample and an agent-led expert review. No participant recruitment, focus group, representative review survey or retention experiment was conducted. Agent agreement is not evidence that all players will be satisfied.

## Design decision

Make finishing a satisfying over the core loop. Mini over contains three rounds; Full over contains six. Put the primary start action before configuration. Explain earned XP in a single result receipt. Local titles recognize completed overs with an answer in every round; they do not claim mastery, geographic rank or monetary returns. Keep sharing voluntary and distinguish practice against a bot from human play.

The proposed return loop is: choose a short match, answer, see a truthful result, keep earned identity, optionally return to a visible finite goal. No new streak loss, forced rematch, random chest, mandatory share or reward modal is needed.

## Review rounds and empirical follow-up

Round 1 established the decisions above from bounded sources. Round 2 inspected `home-screen.tsx`, `play-screen.tsx`, `over-entry.css`, `rivalry-widgets.tsx`, `share-moment.tsx`, `share-moment.mjs`, `over-rewards.mjs`, related progression wiring and locker text. The sharing-label issue was sent as a blocking finding; the invalid-filter recovery and local-scope cues were sent to the home owner. Implementation and executed test evidence must be recorded by their owners after fixes; this document does not upgrade source inspection to tested status.

For a later real-user round, observe people unfamiliar with JHK starting an over, recognizing the opponent, understanding the all-answer bonus, leaving after completion and sharing or declining. Track first-question activation, match completion, voluntary replay and optional satisfaction separately. Share-dialog opening is share intent, not proof of a completed post. Use a real comparison cohort before claiming retention improvement or marketing uplift.

Round 3 follow-up inspected the revised home/play and sharing code. All three findings from this reviewer are resolved in source: local storage scope is visible on home, blocked setup exposes repair beside launch, and result cards use explicit game mode and actual played-round count. No additional blocking design finding remains in the selected scope. This disposition is source-reviewed; automated and observed testing remain independently documented by root.
