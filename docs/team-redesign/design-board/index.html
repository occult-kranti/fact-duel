Team Pavilion.
Your people. Your innings.

A mobile-first squad system with five-player and eleven-player identities, asynchronous team fixtures, and a finite four-team cup.

Open editable flow SVG

Design specification only. Sample team names, scores, rosters and tags below are illustrative, not live activity. Copy can be edited in this canvas; application behavior is implemented and verified separately.

Warm, confident, readable.

Ink carries structure. Saffron marks the next action. Teal means squad identity and completion. Color always has a text or icon equivalent.

Belonging without pressure.

Public team tags, banners and cosmetic milestones make identity visible. A member's contribution is one over, not a demand to stay online. No daily streak loss, paid advantage or mandatory share.

Thumb first. Space second.

One main action per screen. 48–56px controls, 16px mobile gutters, 44px minimum chrome. Desktop rearranges the same actions into a side rail and two columns.
