Contact & corrections

Report a factual error

Include the question identifier, what should change and an original source. Review your draft before posting it to the project issue tracker.

Report a bug or accessibility problem

Give the page, what happened and the browser/device if relevant. Never attach a recovery code, access credential, email address or private profile export.

The issue tracker is public. A private operator/grievance mailbox has not yet been supplied. Do not post private or confidential complaints there. Publishing that private contact and the responsible operator is a prerequisite in the launch checklist.

Manage your own profile

Profile controls provide server-profile export, deletion and recovery-code management. Device-progress controls remain separate. See Privacy for retention and recovery limits.
