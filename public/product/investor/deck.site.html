Play for your club.

A quick cricket quiz. A shared team story.

Mobile-first cricket trivia with solo overs, club rivalries and a reason to meet again.

Evidence & scope

JHK release positioning. No traction, market-size or revenue claim. Target audience and return behavior remain hypotheses to validate.

THE PLAYER NEED

A quick game can start a lasting rivalry.

The hypothesis: friends make the next match meaningful.

Start with a short, understandable over.

Give every player a club identity and a useful role.

End with a result to share and a next match they choose.

Evidence & scope

Audience hypothesis: mobile cricket fans who want a short challenge with friends. This is not a finding from user interviews. Motivation reference: autonomy, competence and relatedness; https://repository.essex.ac.uk/7228/ . Validate using first-match completion and voluntary club return.

THE FIRST OVER

Mini over. Full over. Clear choice.

Choose the time you have before you start.

Mini over — 3 rounds.

Full over — 6 rounds.

Keep the result receipt simple: score, XP and any simulated-coin return.

Evidence & scope

Mode names and round counts are user requirements. Exact match rules and XP values belong to the server and visible game rules; this deck does not promise a payout or XP multiplier. Coins are simulated, with no cash value.

THE CLUB WAR

Prepare together. Play on your time.

A shared clock gives the rivalry a clear beginning and end.

  1. 23h

    PREPARATION

    Invite, choose the playing 5 or 11 and arrange reserves.

  2. 24h

    BATTLE

    The locked lineup plays within the battle window.

  3. Next

    FRESH CHOICE

    Both captains opt in. A new weekly draft needs fresh acceptance.

Both sides can confirm readiness to begin early. Existing live fixtures keep their original deadline.

Evidence & scope

Official reference: https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/about-clan-wars-2.html . CoC has23h preparation and24h battle. JHK adaptation uses trivia matches, playing 5 or 11, club cap22 and fresh weekly draft confirmation. The home side sends; the away side accepts. Recurrence is off by default and needs both captains to opt in; neither side is automatically enrolled. Early start requires the current authorized lineup leadership on both sides; server remains authoritative. Old active fixtures keep their original24h deadline.

LEADERSHIP & IDENTITY

Useful roles. Playful badges.

Authority and personality are separate.

RolePurpose
Captain / Vice-captainLead the club and confirm match decisions.
CoachHelp arrange the lineup; limited authority.
Member / ReserveBelong to the roster; play when selected.
Bottle boy & fun badgesOpt-in identity; remove your own badge.

A badge never grants permission, better answers or extra playing slots.

Evidence & scope

Permissions are enforced on the server. Coach has limited lineup authority, not full club administration. Captain/VC powers remain explicit. Fun badges require player opt-in and self-removal. Avoid making a disparaging role mandatory.

One opponent now. A cup to chase next.

Team fixtures build toward small, understandable tournaments.

8 / 14
CRICKET, IN EVERY OUTCOME

Make the finish feel like cricket.

Joy when you win. Dignity when you lose.

Victory: bat, ball and a short cricket cheer.

Loss: wicket gone; a useful prompt for the next over.

Close finish: a run-out moment, followed by a clear score.

Evidence & scope

Original JHK cricket art direction. Feedback must match the actual result; a close finish is determined from the game rules, not manufactured. Sound starts only after a user gesture, can be muted, and motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Loss feedback avoids shaming and false rewards.

Share the story. Choose the rematch.

A scorecard is the invitation.

    RESULT

    The actual score and club tag become a cricket story.

  2. Try

    FIRST OVER

    The recipient understands the game before joining.

  3. Return

    FRESH DRAFT

    The next match is a choice, with a clear deadline.

Share a cricket result with a club tag and the actual score.

Let the recipient understand the game before asking them to join.

Offer the next match as an opt-in draft with a clear deadline.

Evidence & scope

Growth design hypothesis. No viral coefficient or retention promise. Use native share when available and a copy/download fallback. Exclude private member information from public scorecards. Invites must not automatically accept challenges or enroll players into recurring matches. Future attribution should be consent-aware.
