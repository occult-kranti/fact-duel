# FACT//DUEL — independent first-pass advisor

Reviewed 2026-09-12. Read-only source inspection of the published Curiosity Arcade implementation. No browser, physical-device, accessibility or new market/review research was performed. References below are component/function names, not claims that rendering was observed.

The current product has a credible rules engine and more activity systems than its core identity needs. The redesign should make the first duel, the rivalry and the reason for the result feel unmistakable. Another collection of decorative cards would not address the user's rejection.

## Six decisions, in priority order

| Priority | Existing shortcoming and source evidence | Exact change | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 · 1 | **The first game competes with setup.** `Arena` places a large welcome, a points ticket, opponent tabs, three format choices and a topic selector before its first start button. The settings are already progressively disclosed; the remaining issue is visual order and commitment anxiety. | Place one dominant **Play Lucky Guess** button immediately beneath a specific promise: **Sports. Science. One rival.** Show **Random bot · 1 question · 15 seconds · free** beside it. Make **Challenge a friend**, **Join room** and **Customize duel** secondary routes. | P0 · 2 | **The main promise is split across two identities.** The header says FACT//DUEL while the page is “THE CURIOSITY ARCADE”; the hero, collection and passport voice says “little adventure” and “scientific wonders.” The score-based challenge feels subordinate to an educational scrapbook. | Make **FACT//DUEL** the consistent title, header and result identity. Preserve tap/click release, numeric keyboard control and immutable first answer. | At 390px, the primary reading order is rivals → timer/topic → question → four answers → status. Four typical answers fit without needing to scroll past ancillary metadata; long questions/options expand without clipping or truncation. Answer text is at least 16px, ancillary essential instructions at least 12px, touch targets at least 44px. No moving/reordering targets, answer-key exposure, focus stealing during a question, or premature result reveal. Actual mobile/AT validation remains a distinct gate. |
| P0 · 5 | **Results are emotionally vague and analytically overexposed.** The final headline is “A little bragging is allowed” or “A good rival. A new fact”; `ResultDetails` opens a four-column transport table while explanation is closed. “Too even to settle” can mean both wrong, not close timing. A clear reason is missing. | Lead with literal **YOU WIN / RIVAL WINS / DRAW / MATCH REFUNDED**, then the score and one cause drawn from `round.result.reason`. For one question: **Only you answered correctly**, **Both correct; your reported response was faster**, **Within the 150 ms tie band**, or **Neither answered correctly**. Multi-round verdicts state aggregate score and keep the last-round reason separate. Show a two-player receipt with actual choices, correctness, reported time and bot label. Keep explanation/source one click away; put server transport diagnostics in **How this was decided**. A match win never gets explained solely by the last round. Human times are labelled reported; bot times scheduled; no fake millisecond fairness precision claim. A cancelled match is not presented as an ordinary draw or loss. Primary replay is explicit, creates one fresh room and never silently wagers a different entry. Source opens after settlement only. Diagnostics remain accessible without dominating the receipt. |
| P1 · 6 | **The learning loop collects facts but cannot express a fact-quality concern.** Result/Vault exposes a citation and bookmark; there is no local dispute record. The proposed feedback feature is justified only if its status is honest. | Add **Question issue** after a settled fact: choose **Ambiguous wording / Incorrect answer / Source problem / Other**, optional bounded text, then **Save issue on this device**. ## Design restraint

- A dark/chartreuse skin alone is not enough. The measurable change is fewer pre-play decisions, a dominant question, and a result that can be understood in one glance.
- Keep Passport rewards and discovery available, but remove the mission strip and points ticket from the first-play critical path. The primary loop is choose → duel → understand outcome → optionally learn/replay.
- Reuse the existing protocol. No product copy may promise zero latency, verified human skill, public matchmaking or cross-location fairness proof.
- A visible **Practice bot** or **Lucky Guess · bot** label is a positive trust cue, not small print. The random bot remains 25% correct in expectation with its response scheduled independently.
- Prefer one coherent modular stylesheet over another large appended override layer. The existing CSS has several revisions; ensure the intended theme/touch-size declarations win at every breakpoint.

Run meaningful existing tests plus targeted new state tests. Build/type-check. Mark visual and device claims unverified unless actually inspected.
