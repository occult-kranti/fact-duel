# FACT//DUEL: six prioritized UX requirements

Applied `review-led-game-design` and its evidence contract directly to `trivia-evidence.json` and `learning-evidence.json` on 12 September 2026. Priorities express consequence to this private, four-choice sports/science duel; they are design judgments, not measured complaint frequency. Preserve random bot play, friend rooms, locally earned cosmetics and an optional 3D lobby.

The source artifacts retain 32 observations across eight products, including mixed and positive accounts, with source ledgers and retrieval limits. Full review dates, platform, locator, unknown version/locale fields and official cross-checks remain in those artifacts. Most individual permalinks are unavailable; linked listings must be searched using the retained locator and date. This application inspected the artifacts, not the live products or pages.

For every requirement below: **status = proposed; implementedEvidence = none supplied or inspected**. Owners are suggested roles, not assigned people. Automated acceptance checks and observed acceptance checks are separate gates; neither has been executed here.

## 1. P0 — Keep entry and timed answers free of interruptions

**Evidence and mechanism.** TC02 (6 September 2026) reports reward prompts obstructing trivia; SP02 (26 August 2026) reports in-quiz ads. QP04 (25 September 2023) praises answer comparison but reports transition taps landing unintentionally. These suggest obstruction and stale-input risks, not measured timing disadvantages. [Trivia Crack listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.etermax.preguntados.lite), [Sporcle listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.sporcle.geneva), [Quiz Planet listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quiz-planet/id1466208181?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews).

**Decision.** Offer direct bot/friend entry and a compact rules preview. From question readiness through answer commitment, show the complete question and four stable choices; prevent reward overlays, lobby animation and carried-over pointer/key events. Start time only at the declared readiness boundary. Show the committed choice immediately; explanations follow commitment. Do not require extra reward screens to continue.

**Owner/dependencies.** Gameplay UI; question lifecycle and input handling. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: one commitment per question; held/repeated input cannot answer the next question; overlays cannot mount in timed states. Observed: fast taps, keyboard activation and actual touch transitions produce the intended choice. **Remaining risk.** Animation timing and physical input require real-device observation; removing overlays alone does not prove fair latency.

## 2. P0 — Make interruption, settlement and recovery understandable

**Evidence and mechanism.** TC01 (2 April 2025) reports interrupted friend play; SP01 (4 September 2026) praises variety but reports missing results; L11 (11 December 2025) reports lost study progress. QP01 (8 August 2026) reports timeout losses; its developer reply confirms intended inactivity timeout, not duration or the exact incident. [Trivia Crack listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.etermax.preguntados.lite), [Sporcle listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.sporcle.geneva), [Quizlet listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.quizlet.com?page=4), [Quiz Planet listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.lotum.quizplanet).

**Decision.** Declare disconnection, backgrounding, timeout, tie and quit behavior before play. Restore committed answers and a durable result receipt after reload; show pending/reconnecting/expired states explicitly. Retrying a write must not duplicate settlement. Persist the result before optional celebration. State any simulated-currency change plainly if that currency already exists.

**Owner/dependencies.** Match-state engineering; match IDs, authoritative timing, persistence and room protocol. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: refresh, duplicate commit, interruption around settlement and failed storage preserve or explicitly reject the same outcome. Observed: two independent clients reconnect across separate networks and see a consistent result. **Remaining risk.** Local or fixture success does not demonstrate friend-room WAN behavior; a visible pending state is required when recovery is unresolved.

## 3. P0 — Let players inspect answers and understand repeated content

**Evidence and mechanism.** QP01 raises slow corrections; L10 (15 May 2026) alleges Welsh errors without supplying items; QD02 (17 September 2021) objects to close repeats; L03 (6 June 2026) objects to repetition presented as new. QP04 values answer comparison. L13 describes insufficient explanations in a November 2025 experience; L14 praises practical examples in September 2026. [Quiz Planet listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.lotum.quizplanet), [Quizlet listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.quizlet.com?page=2), [QuizDuel listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizduel-trivia-quiz-game/id1484354626?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews), [Duolingo listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com?page=9), [Brilliant listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brilliant.org).

**Decision.** After commitment, expose the answer, a concise rationale, source link, question version and report action. Give each item a stable content-family ID; avoid near-term competitive repeats where the pool permits, and disclose pool exhaustion. Label intentional repetition as review. Retain reported item/version and affected match IDs; acknowledge local-only reports honestly and disclose reviewed corrections without silently rewriting a settled result.

**Owner/dependencies.** Content owner with gameplay engineering; verified question bank, versioning, exposure policy and report workflow. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: required provenance fields, family-aware selection and report receipts. Observed: editorial item verification and players finding/understanding explanations and report status. **Remaining risk.** Opening an explanation proves only opening; a recall attempt proves only an attempt. Neither demonstrates mastery. No supplied evidence establishes AI authorship of L10's alleged errors or the current competitor repeat rate.

## 4. P0 — Keep ordinary controls complete, with optional 3D enhancement

**Evidence and mechanism.** L02 (6 June 2026; experience 3 May) reports inaccessible input. L05 (2 March 2026) finds distant host-screen content hard to read; official help confirms an existing player-screen setting, so the transferable issue is discoverability and own-device readability. L09 (19 May 2026) reports sluggish study interaction. These are adjacent risks; none tests a 3D lobby. [Duolingo listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com?page=9), [Kahoot listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kahoot.com?page=7), [Kahoot player-screen help](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003197928-How-to-enable-See-questions-on-participant-s-screen-in-Kahoot-live-games), [Quizlet listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.quizlet.com?page=2).

**Decision.** Provide semantic, stationary controls for every action, complete questions on each player's device, visible focus and an equally usable static lobby. Make 3D opt-in; respect reduced-motion/data preferences. Stop GPU work during questions; cap pixel density and animation duration and clean up rendering resources on exit. Keep an untimed four-choice practice route visible.

**Owner/dependencies.** Frontend/accessibility owner; semantic UI, preference handling and renderer lifecycle. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: 3D failure leaves every action available; no render loop remains in timed states. Observed: keyboard, screen reader, touch, 200% zoom, reduced motion and physical low-end-device rendering/input checks. **Remaining risk.** Source inspection cannot establish accessibility, frame rate, battery cost or input responsiveness. Optional 3D is a scope-preserving proposal, not a review-proven benefit.

## 5. P1 — Explain opponents and make friend-room states legible

**Evidence and mechanism.** QD01 (10 June 2025) values fast matching; its belief in human opponents does not verify opponent type. QP02 (27 August 2026) reports unclear identity; QP03 (29 March 2025) reports abandoned games cluttering the list; QP06 (14 January 2024) reports accidental nudges. L06 (3 March 2026) enjoys easy hosting and competition. [QuizDuel listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizduel-trivia-quiz-game/id1484354626?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews), [Quiz Planet Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.lotum.quizplanet), [Quiz Planet iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quiz-planet/id1466208181?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews), [Kahoot listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kahoot.com?page=7).

**Decision.** Label random opponents as bots before starting. Show the friend-room code, persistent display identity, connected/ready state and who may start. Explain live timing; expose join retry, leave and expired-room recovery. Keep results/history read-only with respect to social actions. Make rematches deliberate and make completed/abandoned rooms dismissible without removing earned cosmetics.

**Owner/dependencies.** Social UX with room engineering; identity, readiness and room lifecycle. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: invalid/expired/full-room responses and read-only history. Observed: a first-time host and guest join, identify each other, recover a failed join and leave without confusion. **Remaining risk.** Bot labeling is an ethical/product inference; neither bot quality nor hosting ease is established by the panel. Do not claim QuizDuel lacks teams: retained official help documents them.

## 6. P1 — Reward finite activity honestly and preserve local unlocks

**Evidence and mechanism.** L01 (1 October 2025) describes energy restrictions and progression disruption; L03 objects to misleading novelty; L04 (3 June 2026) dislikes pressure while crediting improvement; L06 enjoys competition. SP03 (21 August 2026) questions timer/badge restrictions. These support player choice and clear reward meaning, not a universal rejection of gamification. [First-person Duolingo account](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/), [Duolingo listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com?page=9), [Kahoot listing](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kahoot.com?page=7), [Sporcle listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.sporcle.geneva).

**Decision.** Use optional, finite missions with visible stopping points and a visible untimed earning route. Award cosmetics for accurately named activity, independent of correctness, odds, question access and simulated currency. No punitive expiration or automatic rematch. Separate permanent unlock records from capped activity history; use stable family IDs and idempotent event IDs. Specify schema migration, transactional concurrent writes, failed-storage feedback, export/import, deletion and explicit reset. Say that local unlocks can be lost through cleared storage unless exported; never promise account/cloud persistence.

**Owner/dependencies.** Progression UX with storage engineering; event definitions, durable unlock store and migration/reset policy. **Acceptance / validationType.** Automated: repeated events and concurrent tabs cannot double-count or erase unlocks; history pruning and migrations retain earned state; failed writes are not shown as saved; export/import round-trip works. Observed: players can explain what earned a cosmetic, find the untimed route and stop comfortably. **Remaining risk.** Durable state needs executed failure tests; any learning, motivation or retention benefit needs a defined study, not review inference.

## Claim boundary and release use

Permitted wording: “Selected reviewers reported these experiences; they suggest these risks for FACT//DUEL.” Official pages support only their documented feature scope. The purposive, English-heavy panel cannot establish prevalence, causal retention effects, market rankings, current universal defects, payment intent or competitor bot usage. Dates range from historical incidents to recent publications, all review versions are unknown, and mutable listing access limits reproducibility.

Keep the six requirements as proposed until implementation evidence exists. Use source-reviewed only for inspected code, automated-tested only for executed checks, and observed-tested only for the specific recorded device, input, accessibility or network scenario. Human observation remains necessary for discoverability, clarity, touch behavior, room recovery and perceived pressure; delayed-recall measurement is required before claiming learning gains. Billing and renewal observations are retained context, not grounds to add payments or account systems to this private prototype.
