Beta terms

Version: beta-1. These are the current rules of participation in this adult testing beta. The operator must complete its legal identity, private support contact and jurisdiction review before a general public or monetized launch.

Who can play

This beta is for adults aged 18 or older. Practice does not require signup. Human duels create a device profile with a public nickname after your age and beta-terms confirmation. Email is optional; add an address only if you are entitled to provide it. The age declaration is self-attested; the beta does not verify identity or email ownership. Do not impersonate another person. Keep your browser access and recovery codes private. Read the privacy notice before creating the profile.

Free simulated coins

The games are free to play. Coins are in-game scores, with no cash value, purchase, deposit, redemption or prize. An optional stake uses only these free coins and defaults to zero. Zero balance never prevents a zero-stake duel. Coins, profiles and rewards must not be sold, transferred for money or used to promise anything of monetary value outside the game.

Human duels and settlement

Quick play looks for a human for up to five seconds. If none joins, a clearly labelled local practice bot starts with zero stake. Practice results do not affect human rankings or server coin balances. Private invitations remain human-only.

New online duels offer Mini over with three rounds or Full over with six, with a 30-second limit for each question. Previously created five-round rooms retain their original rules. The server locks the first accepted answer and decides the result using correct-answer count and server-measured total response time. A difference within 120 milliseconds is a draw. Network delay can affect timing; equal geographic latency is not promised.

Both players confirm the same optional stake before it is reserved. The winner receives the combined stake. A draw, pre-start cancellation or applicable system cancellation refunds reserved stakes. After both players are ready, leaving or deleting a profile forfeits the stake to the opponent. One player absent for more than 90 seconds while the opponent remains active forfeits; when both are absent, reserved stakes are refunded. Completion rewards are separate from the pot and follow the visible qualification and daily limits.

Club wars and cups

A player can belong to one club of up to 22 members. Each club has a fixed playing format of five or eleven; selected players contribute and reserves do not score for that fixture. Captains manage the club, Vice Captains arrange optional matches, and Coaches manage lineups. Optional fun badges grant no permissions and require the recipient’s consent. New accepted fixtures have 23 hours of preparation, when selected players can be substituted, followed by 24 hours of battle with locked player identities. Both captains or vice captains can explicitly start battle early; a lineup change clears readiness. Each selected player gets one Mini or Full over. Existing live fixtures keep their original clocks. Weekly rematches are drafts requiring fresh confirmation from both clubs, and either club can stop the series. The first accepted answer is final. Correct answers add to team score; tied scores use total server-measured answer time, with every wrong or missing answer counted as 30 seconds. A direct fixture can draw. Four-team cups have two semi-finals and a final, with preparation before each new stage. An exact score-and-time cup tie advances the lower original seed, assigned by entry order and shown before play. An incomplete selected sheet when preparation ends cancels the fixture or cup without awards.

Team matches have no entry cost or wager. Team points, levels and banner unlocks are cosmetic and do not improve answer time, question access, personal rank or coin balance. Recruiting cups expire after 48 hours and can be left or cancelled by captains before starting. Fixture and cup status is reconciled when an authenticated team request is made; an offline screen does not continuously settle results. Do not put sensitive personal information in public team names.

Fair play and conduct

Do not automate play, collude to farm coins, exploit bugs, harass other players or use abusive or misleading nicknames. The beta may be paused to fix faults. Leaderboard labels and achievements describe game activity; they do not verify a person's knowledge, identity, political beliefs or official status.

Questions and achievements

Sports and science questions include source receipts. Facts and historical scope can be corrected; report the question identifier and an original source. Medals and leaderboard titles are game achievements, not an academic or professional qualification.

Ads and sharing

Ads are currently disabled. If configured later, potential video/interstitial breaks occur between games, subject to consent, availability and provider rules. Ads never decide an answer, score or stake. Sharing is voluntary; include only content or profile images you have permission to use.

Help and data controls

Use profile controls for recovery-code management, export or deletion, and Contact & corrections for the available reporting route. No verified-email recovery is available in this beta. These product rules do not claim regulatory approval or remove rights you have under applicable law.
