  1. Which fast bowler did Harbhajan Singh slap after an IPL match at Mohali on 25 April 2008, earning a ban for the rest of the season?

    1. S Sreesanth
    2. Irfan Pathan
    3. Ishant Sharma
    4. RP Singh
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: S Sreesanth

    The bowler, then with Kings XI Punjab, was filmed in tears after beating Mumbai Indians; the BCCI added a separate five-ODI ban.

    Source: Wikipedia: S. Sreesanth · Difficulty: simple

  2. Which player from the losing side was named player of the match in the 2009 IPL final in Johannesburg?

    1. Herschelle Gibbs
    2. RP Singh
    3. Anil Kumble
    4. Jacques Kallis
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Anil Kumble

    Kumble, captaining Bangalore, took 4 for 16, but RCB finished on 137 for 9 chasing 144.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2009 Indian Premier League final · Difficulty: extreme

  3. Chennai Super Kings won their first IPL title in 2010. Which team did they beat in the final?

    1. Royal Challengers Bangalore
    2. Deccan Chargers
    3. Kolkata Knight Riders
    4. Mumbai Indians
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Mumbai Indians

    CSK made 168 for 5 and won by 22 runs at the DY Patil Stadium on 25 April 2010, in Mumbai's first final.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2010 Indian Premier League final · Difficulty: simple

  4. Who was player of the match in the 2010 IPL final with an unbeaten 57 off 35 balls for Chennai Super Kings?

    1. Suresh Raina
    2. MS Dhoni
    3. Albie Morkel
    4. Murali Vijay
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Suresh Raina

    Sachin Tendulkar top-scored for Mumbai with 48 and finished the season with the Orange Cap for 618 runs.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2010 Indian Premier League final · Difficulty: expert

  5. In which year did the BCCI suspend founding IPL commissioner Lalit Modi, immediately after that season's final?

    1. 2008
    2. 2010
    3. 2011
    4. 2013
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 2010

    He faced 22 charges including bid rigging; a BCCI committee expelled him for life in September 2013.

    Source: Wikipedia: Lalit Modi · Difficulty: extreme

  6. The Lodha committee suspended Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals from the IPL for which two seasons?

    1. 2014 and 2015
    2. 2015 and 2016
    3. 2017 and 2018
    4. 2016 and 2017
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 2016 and 2017

    The verdict of 14 July 2015 also imposed life bans on Gurunath Meiyappan and Rajasthan co-owner Raj Kundra.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2013 Indian Premier League spot-fixing case · Difficulty: expert

  7. How many Test five-wicket hauls did R Ashwin finish with, the second-most in the history of the format?

    1. 29
    2. 33
    3. 35
    4. 37
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 37

    He was ICC Cricketer of the Year in 2016, received the Padma Shri in January 2025 and retired from the IPL in August 2025.

    Source: Wikipedia: Ravichandran Ashwin · Difficulty: extreme

  8. Which IPL franchise played only one season, in 2011, before the BCCI terminated it?

    1. Pune Warriors India
    2. Deccan Chargers
    3. Kochi Tuskers Kerala
    4. Gujarat Lions
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Kochi Tuskers Kerala

    Captained by Mahela Jayawardene, Kochi finished eighth of ten; the franchise was terminated in September 2011 over an unpaid bank guarantee.

    Source: Wikipedia: Kochi Tuskers Kerala · Difficulty: simple

  9. Gurunath Meiyappan, the Chennai Super Kings team principal arrested in the 2013 case, was related to BCCI president N. Srinivasan how?

    1. Nephew
    2. Brother-in-law
    3. Son-in-law
    4. Son
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Son-in-law

    The Supreme Court-appointed Mudgal committee found in February 2014 that Meiyappan had violated the IPL anti-corruption code.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2013 Indian Premier League spot-fixing case · Difficulty: expert

  10. Which group owned Pune Warriors India, the franchise that withdrew from the IPL in May 2013 over its fee?

    1. Reliance
    2. Videocon
    3. Adani
    4. Sahara
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Sahara

    Sahara paid about 1,702 crore rupees for the franchise in 2010; Pune won only 12 of 46 matches.

    Source: Wikipedia: Pune Warriors India · Difficulty: extreme
