  1. Bobby Thomson’s 1951 "Shot Heard Round the World" won the pennant for which club?

    1. Brooklyn Dodgers
    2. Boston Braves
    3. New York Yankees
    4. New York Giants
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: New York Giants

    Russ Hodges’ call, "The Giants win the pennant!", capped a playoff against Brooklyn.

    Source: SABR: Bobby Thomson biography · Difficulty: simple

  2. The fixed 1919 World Series was played under which unusual format?

    1. Best-of-nine
    2. Best-of-five
    3. Best-of-seven
    4. Best-of-eleven
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Best-of-nine

    Cincinnati clinched in Game 8; Joe Jackson hit the Series’ only home run in that game.

    Source: SABR: Shoeless Joe Jackson biography · Difficulty: expert

  3. Which White Sox ace hit Reds leadoff man Morrie Rath to signal the gamblers that the fix was on?

    1. Lefty Williams
    2. Eddie Cicotte
    3. Red Faber
    4. Dickey Kerr
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Eddie Cicotte

    Cicotte had won 29 games in 1919 and later confessed to the grand jury.

    Source: SABR: Eddie Cicotte biography · Difficulty: expert

  4. Which newspaper’s reporter, Charley Owens, first printed the "Say it ain’t so, Joe" anecdote?

    1. Chicago Tribune
    2. Chicago Daily News
    3. Chicago Herald and Examiner
    4. New York World
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Chicago Daily News

    Jackson himself denied the exchange outside the grand jury ever happened.

    Source: SABR: Shoeless Joe Jackson biography · Difficulty: extreme

  5. Which Dodgers reliever threw the pitch Bobby Thomson hit for the 1951 pennant?

    1. Don Newcombe
    2. Preacher Roe
    3. Ralph Branca
    4. Carl Erskine
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Ralph Branca

    Branca had relieved Don Newcombe with one out in the ninth; Thomson hit his second pitch.

    Source: Baseball-Reference: Dodgers at Giants, October 3, 1951 · Difficulty: expert

  6. How many intentional walks did Barry Bonds draw in 2004, the record season in which he walked 232 times?

    1. 96
    2. 104
    3. 120
    4. 133
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 120

    Bonds posted a .609 on-base percentage that year and won his seventh MVP.

    Source: Baseball-Reference: Barry Bonds · Difficulty: extreme

  7. Off which Cubs pitcher did Mark McGwire hit his record-breaking 62nd home run on September 8, 1998?

    1. Kerry Wood
    2. Kevin Tapani
    3. Rod Beck
    4. Steve Trachsel
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Steve Trachsel

    The line drive down the left-field line came in the fourth inning at Busch Stadium.

    Source: Baseball-Reference: Cubs at Cardinals, September 8, 1998 · Difficulty: expert

  8. Against which team did Shohei Ohtani hit three homers and steal two bases to reach 50-50 in September 2024?

    1. Colorado Rockies
    2. San Diego Padres
    3. Arizona Diamondbacks
    4. Miami Marlins
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Miami Marlins

    He went 6-for-6 with 10 RBIs in Miami on September 19, 2024, finishing the day at 51 homers and 51 steals.

    Source: Baseball-Reference: Dodgers at Marlins, September 19, 2024 · Difficulty: expert

  9. Which former US Senator authored the December 2007 report into steroid use in baseball?

    1. George Mitchell
    2. Bill Bradley
    3. Jim Bunning
    4. Tom Harkin
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: George Mitchell

    The Mitchell Report, dated December 13, 2007, ran to more than 400 pages.

    Source: MLB: The Mitchell Report · Difficulty: expert

  10. Which banned Black Sox third baseman hit .324 in the Series, took no money, and petitioned for reinstatement until 1953?

    1. Buck Weaver
    2. Chick Gandil
    3. Swede Risberg
    4. Fred McMullin
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Buck Weaver

    Weaver was expelled for knowing of the fix and not reporting it; his last plea went to Ford Frick.

    Source: SABR: Buck Weaver biography · Difficulty: extreme
