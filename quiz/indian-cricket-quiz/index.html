  1. Which Australian bowler took 5 for 8 as India collapsed to 36 all out at Adelaide in December 2020?

    1. Pat Cummins
    2. Josh Hazlewood
    3. Mitchell Starc
    4. Nathan Lyon
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Josh Hazlewood

    Australia went 1-0 up, yet India won the series 2-1 and Pat Cummins was still named player of the series.

    Source: Wikipedia: Indian cricket team in Australia in 2020-21 · Difficulty: extreme

  2. For how many runs were India bowled out in their second innings of the day-night Adelaide Test in December 2020, their lowest total in Test cricket?

    1. 36
    2. 42
    3. 47
    4. 58
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 36

    No Indian batsman reached double figures, a first in Test history, and Australia chased the small target to win by eight wickets.

    Source: Wikipedia: Indian cricket team in Australia in 2020-21 · Difficulty: simple

  3. Who was unbeaten on 89 when India completed the 328-run chase to win the Gabba Test in January 2021?

    1. Shubman Gill
    2. Cheteshwar Pujara
    3. Washington Sundar
    4. Rishabh Pant
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Rishabh Pant

    Pant faced 138 balls and sealed the win with a boundary to long-off; it was the first Test Australia had lost at the Gabba since 1988.

    Source: cricket.com.au: Indian summer, Gabba streak ends with classic Test win · Difficulty: simple

  4. Who captained India in the last three Tests of the 2020-21 series in Australia after Virat Kohli returned home on paternity leave?

    1. Rohit Sharma
    2. Cheteshwar Pujara
    3. Ajinkya Rahane
    4. KL Rahul
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Ajinkya Rahane

    Rahane also made 112 in the Boxing Day Test at Melbourne, which India won by eight wickets to level the series.

    Source: Wikipedia: Indian cricket team in Australia in 2020-21 · Difficulty: simple

  5. Which body set aside Sreesanth's BCCI life ban in March 2019 and asked the board to reconsider its length?

    1. Supreme Court of India
    2. Kerala High Court
    3. Delhi High Court
    4. Court of Arbitration for Sport
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Supreme Court of India

    The BCCI ombudsman then cut the ban to seven years in August 2019, so Sreesanth could play again from September 2020.

    Source: Wikipedia: Sreesanth · Difficulty: extreme

  6. Which team beat India in the inaugural World Test Championship final at Southampton in June 2021?

    1. Australia
    2. New Zealand
    3. England
    4. South Africa
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: New Zealand

    The match went into its reserve sixth day; Kane Williamson and Ross Taylor were unbeaten as the target of 139 was reached for two wickets.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2021 ICC World Test Championship final · Difficulty: simple

  7. Whose unbeaten 60 off 32 balls earned the player-of-the-match award as Mumbai Indians won their first IPL title in the 2013 final?

    1. Rohit Sharma
    2. Kieron Pollard
    3. Lasith Malinga
    4. Harbhajan Singh
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Kieron Pollard

    Mumbai recovered from 52 for 4 to 148 for 9 and beat Chennai by 23 runs at Eden Gardens on 26 May 2013.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2013 Indian Premier League final · Difficulty: extreme

  8. Who took 5 for 73 in Australia's second innings at the Gabba in January 2021, his first five-wicket haul in Tests?

    1. Mohammed Siraj
    2. Shardul Thakur
    3. T Natarajan
    4. Navdeep Saini
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Mohammed Siraj

    Natarajan and Washington Sundar were both making their Test debuts in that match, with Bumrah, Ashwin and Jadeja all injured.

    Source: Wikipedia: Indian cricket team in Australia in 2020-21 · Difficulty: expert

  9. Virat Kohli scored a record 973 runs in IPL 2016. How many centuries did he make that season?

    1. Two
    2. Three
    3. Four
    4. Five
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Four

    He averaged 81.08 and won both the Orange Cap and the most valuable player award, but RCB lost the final.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2016 Indian Premier League · Difficulty: simple

  10. At which ground did India lose the 2023 World Test Championship final to Australia?

    1. Lord's
    2. Edgbaston
    3. Southampton
    4. The Oval
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: The Oval

    Australia won by 209 runs in June 2023 to claim their first World Test Championship title.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2023 ICC World Test Championship final · Difficulty: simple
