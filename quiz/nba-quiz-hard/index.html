  1. Who coached the 1972 USA team that voted unanimously to refuse its silver medals?

    1. Henry Iba
    2. Dean Smith
    3. Bob Knight
    4. John Thompson
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Henry Iba

    The defeat ended a 63-game Olympic winning streak and a run of seven straight golds; the US protest was rejected by FIBA.

    Source: USA Basketball (archived): 1972 Olympic Games · Difficulty: extreme

  2. Which country beat John Thompson’s USA 82-76 in the 1988 Seoul semifinal, the last Olympics before NBA players?

    1. Yugoslavia
    2. Soviet Union
    3. Australia
    4. Brazil
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Soviet Union

    The Soviets took gold, Yugoslavia silver and the USA bronze; four years later the Dream Team arrived.

    Source: USA Basketball (archived): 1988 Olympic Games · Difficulty: extreme

  3. Whose steal of a James Worthy pass in Game 2 of the 1984 Finals turned that series for Boston?

    1. Dennis Johnson
    2. Danny Ainge
    3. Gerald Henderson
    4. M.L. Carr
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Gerald Henderson

    The Lakers led by two with 18 seconds left; Henderson’s layup forced overtime and Boston won the series in seven.

    Source: NBA.com: Season Review 1983-84 · Difficulty: extreme

  4. Who was the only college player on the 1992 Dream Team?

    1. Shaquille O’Neal
    2. Alonzo Mourning
    3. Grant Hill
    4. Christian Laettner
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Christian Laettner

    The Duke forward, the best college player of 1991-92, completed a roster of eleven NBA stars.

    Source: USA Basketball (archived): 1992 Olympic Games · Difficulty: extreme

  5. Who was MVP of the first WNBA All-Star Game, played in New York in 1999?

    1. Tina Thompson
    2. Cynthia Cooper
    3. Teresa Weatherspoon
    4. Lisa Leslie
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Lisa Leslie

    The West won 79-61; Leslie won the honour again in 2001 and 2002.

    Source: WNBA: All-Time All-Star Game results · Difficulty: extreme

  6. Which franchise won its only WNBA title in 2005, the first year of the best-of-five Finals?

    1. Sacramento Monarchs
    2. Detroit Shock
    3. Connecticut Sun
    4. Charlotte Sting
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Sacramento Monarchs

    Yolanda Griffith was Finals MVP as Sacramento beat Connecticut 3-1.

    Source: WNBA: All-Time WNBA Champions · Difficulty: extreme

  7. Which college did Scottie Pippen attend?

    1. Memphis
    2. Central Arkansas
    3. Arkansas
    4. Arkansas State
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Central Arkansas

    Pippen played college basketball at Central Arkansas.

    Source: Basketball Hall of Fame: Pippen · Difficulty: extreme

  8. In what position did the USA finish at the 2019 FIBA World Cup?

    1. 4th
    2. 5th
    3. 9th
    4. 7th
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 7th

    Australia, Serbia and the Czech Republic all placed above a US squad missing most of its stars.

    Source: FIBA: Basketball World Cup 2019 · Difficulty: extreme

  9. Which country hosted and won the first FIBA World Championship in 1950?

    1. Brazil
    2. Argentina
    3. USA
    4. Uruguay
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Argentina

    The USA finished second and Chile third; Argentina would not win the event again.

    Source: FIBA: Basketball World Cup editions · Difficulty: extreme

  10. In which year was Michael Jordan individually inducted into the Basketball Hall of Fame?

    1. 2009
    2. 2008
    3. 2010
    4. 2011
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 2009

    His individual induction was in 2009.

    Source: Basketball Hall of Fame: Jordan · Difficulty: extreme
