  1. In how many Tests did Azharuddin captain India?

    1. 35
    2. 41
    3. 47
    4. 53
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 47

    He won 14 and lost 14, and is the only Indian captain to have led at three World Cups: 1992, 1996 and 1999.

    Source: Wikipedia: List of India national cricket captains · Difficulty: extreme

  2. The 1992 World Cup in Australia and New Zealand introduced coloured clothing and white balls. Who won it?

    1. England
    2. Pakistan
    3. Australia
    4. West Indies
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Pakistan

    Pakistan beat England by 22 runs in the final at the MCG; India finished seventh with two wins from eight matches.

    Source: Wikipedia: 1992 Cricket World Cup · Difficulty: simple

  3. At which South African ground did Sachin Tendulkar make his 98 off 75 balls against Pakistan in the 2003 World Cup?

    1. Durban
    2. Johannesburg
    3. Centurion
    4. Cape Town
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Centurion

    India chased 274 with more than four overs to spare after Saeed Anwar had made 101 for Pakistan; Shoaib Akhtar finally had Tendulkar caught off a bouncer.

    Source: Wisden: Tendulkar hard slashes Shoaib Akhtar for iconic six · Difficulty: simple

  4. Australia beat India by one run at Brisbane in the 1992 World Cup after rain revised India's target. Which Australian made 90?

    1. Dean Jones
    2. Allan Border
    3. David Boon
    4. Steve Waugh
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Dean Jones

    Azharuddin made 93 in reply, but India's target had become 236 off 47 overs and they fell one short.

    Source: Wikipedia: 1992 Cricket World Cup round-robin stage · Difficulty: extreme

  5. How many runs did Sachin Tendulkar score across the 2003 World Cup on his way to the player of the tournament award?

    1. 587
    2. 673
    3. 623
    4. 712
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 673

    He played 11 matches and led the run charts by a distance even though Australia won the final.

    Source: Wikipedia: 2003 Cricket World Cup · Difficulty: extreme

  6. How many runs did Yuvraj Singh score in his sixth-wicket stand with Mohammad Kaif in the 2002 NatWest final?

    1. 58
    2. 75
    3. 69
    4. 87
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: 69

    Yuvraj faced 63 balls and the pair added 121 before Paul Collingwood dismissed him; India won with three balls to spare.

    Source: Wikipedia: Yuvraj Singh · Difficulty: extreme

  7. Which team did India beat by 35 runs in the 1996 Titan Cup final at the Wankhede Stadium?

    1. Australia
    2. Sri Lanka
    3. Pakistan
    4. South Africa
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: South Africa

    South Africa had won all six league matches; Anil Kumble's 4 for 25 was the match-winning spell under Tendulkar's captaincy.

    Source: Wikipedia: 1996 Titan Cup · Difficulty: expert

  8. In which Test of the 1989-90 series was Tendulkar hit on the nose by a Waqar Younis bouncer and refused to leave the field?

    1. Karachi
    2. Sialkot
    3. Lahore
    4. Faisalabad
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Sialkot

    It was the fourth and final Test; he declined medical help and kept batting as his nose bled.

    Source: Wikipedia: Sachin Tendulkar · Difficulty: extreme

  9. Who scored 233 and an unbeaten 72 as India won the Adelaide Test of December 2003 by four wickets?

    1. VVS Laxman
    2. Sachin Tendulkar
    3. Rahul Dravid
    4. Sourav Ganguly
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Rahul Dravid

    It was India's first Test win in Australia for 23 years; Dravid square-cut the winning boundary and kissed the crest on his cap.

    Source: Wikipedia: Rahul Dravid · Difficulty: simple

  10. At which ground did Anil Kumble take all ten Pakistan wickets in an innings in February 1999?

    1. Eden Gardens
    2. Chepauk
    3. Feroz Shah Kotla
    4. Wankhede Stadium
    Show the answer

    Correct answer: Feroz Shah Kotla

    His 10 for 74 set up a 212-run win that squared the two-Test series 1-1.

    Source: Wikipedia: Anil Kumble · Difficulty: simple
